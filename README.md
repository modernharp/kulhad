## What this is

Kulhad gives you a page at `kulhad.co/yourname` carrying your UPI QR code.

A supporter picks an amount, writes a message, scans the QR from GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM, and pays. The money goes from their bank to yours over UPI. Kulhad sits outside that line: we don't process the payment, we can't see your account, and there is no wallet and no float. That is why there is no commission to take, nothing to withdraw, and no payout to wait for.

Claim a name, add one UPI ID. That is the whole setup.

## Features

### Getting paid

- **0% commission, on every plan.** Supporters pay your UPI ID directly, so there is no cut and no gateway fee.
- **Money goes straight to your bank.** Bank to bank over UPI, the same day.
- **Nothing to withdraw.** The money is already yours the moment it lands.
- **Works with the UPI ID you already have.** Your bank handle, GPay, PhonePe or Paytm.
- **Works in any UPI app.** GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and the rest.
- **No KYC, no GST number, no documents.** No merchant account, no card reader, no monthly hardware rent.

### Your page

- **A link that is yours.** `kulhad.co/yourname`, or your own domain on Premium.
- **A wall of thank-yous.** Approved messages sit on your page with your replies underneath.
- **Goals people watch fill.** Set a target for the mic, the trip, the studio month. Supporters see how close it is getting.
- **A QR you can print.** Download it, tape it beside the counter, stand it on a table. Works with no phone in your hand.
- **Seven languages.** English, हिन्दी, Hinglish, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు and മലയാളം.
- **Voice notes, once you approve them.** Fifteen seconds of audio, published only after you've heard it and said yes. *(Premium)*

### Control and privacy

- **Approval before anything publishes.** Nothing appears on your page until you say so.
- **You own your supporters.** Download the full list as a spreadsheet whenever you like. We never email your audience.
- **Supporters never sign up.** No account, no card details, ever.
- **Anonymous by default.** Name and email are both optional, and blank means genuinely anonymous.
- **No sale of personal data.**
- **Delete your page in one tap.**

## How it works

| | |
|---|---|
| **1. They pick an amount** | Three amounts and a box for anything else, plus a message. |
| **2. Kulhad shows a QR** | A four-character reference code goes into the payment note before the QR opens. |
| **3. They pay and tap "I've paid"** | Money moves from their bank to yours. Nothing passes through us. |
| **4. You check and approve** | Match the amount and the code in your bank app, then publish the message. |

Your bank message already tells you the amount and the time, which is usually enough. The reference code settles the one case it can't: two people sending the same amount minutes apart.

## Common questions

**Do you take a cut?**
No. Supporters pay your UPI ID directly, so there is no commission and no gateway fee. You never wait for a payout, because there is nothing for us to pay out.

**How do you know someone actually paid?**
We don't, and we'd rather say so plainly. We cannot see your bank account. You check your own bank app or SMS for the amount and the code, and approve it yourself.

**What if someone taps "I've paid" without paying?**
The entry waits in your pending list and you simply don't approve it. Nobody else ever sees it, and it deletes itself after a few days.

**Do I need a business account, KYC or anything to accept money legally?**
No. Any personal UPI ID works. It receives money the same way it does when a friend repays you for dinner. Keep a record of what arrives, because it is income and it is yours to declare. If it grows to a size your bank treats as business, ask them for a merchant UPI ID and paste it into settings.

**Can someone outside India support me?**
No. UPI is domestic, so a supporter needs an Indian bank account. If most of your audience is abroad, a card-based platform will serve you better than this one.
